World material pack for Agent Deathmatch

Source surface photographs: ambientCG Metal032 and Concrete034, CC0 1.0.
https://ambientcg.com/view?id=Metal032
https://ambientcg.com/view?id=Concrete034
https://docs.ambientcg.com/license/

Plate seams, chamfers, fastener recesses, material color treatments, aligned
normal/occlusion/roughness/metalness maps and 1K variants were authored for
this project. See manifest.json for source archive and output SHA-256 hashes.

Encoded with the official Khronos KTX-Software 4.4.2 encoder, UASTC and
Zstandard, with complete mip chains. Regenerate using
scripts/maps/prepare-materials.py; source archives are cached outside public/.
